Key takeaways CRM (Customer Relationship Management) manages customer data such as contact details, purchase history, and interaction records to help teams organise sales, marketing, and service activities. CX (Customer Experience) focuses on how customers perceive every interaction with a brand across the entire journey. CRM provides structured data, while CX uses insights to design better, more personalised experiences.
