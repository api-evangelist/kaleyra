Key takeaways Voice AI is transforming customer interactions by enabling natural, real-time conversations that go beyond traditional IVRs and scripted automation. Modern systems can understand intent, maintain context and support task completion across channels. Advances in speech-to-speech architectures and low-latency processing are making conversations faster and more human-like, improving customer experiences and reducing friction.
