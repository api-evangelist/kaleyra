Key takeaways Selecting the right voice AI platform requires evaluating business outcomes, not just product features. Conversational accuracy, response time, security, and integration capabilities are among the most important factors when comparing vendors. A successful deployment depends on scalability, enterprise support, and transparent pricing as much as the underlying technology.
