Key takeaways AI virtual assistants and voice AI agents serve different purposes. Virtual assistants focus on self-service and information delivery, while Voice AI agents support real-time conversations and task execution. Voice AI agents offer advanced capabilities such as context retention, goal-oriented interactions and workflow integration through modern platforms.
