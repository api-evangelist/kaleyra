Key takeaways Modern AI call centre platforms combine automation, analytics and conversational technologies to improve customer experiences and operational efficiency. Real-time agent assistance, intelligent call routing and post-call analytics help organisations reduce effort for both customers and agents. Industries such as BFSI, healthcare, retail and telecom are increasingly using voice AI customer service capabilities to support large-scale interactions.
