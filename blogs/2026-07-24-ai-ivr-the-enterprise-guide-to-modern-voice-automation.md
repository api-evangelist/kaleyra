Key takeaways AI IVR is helping organisations move away from rigid menu-driven systems and deliver more natural, conversational customer experiences through intent recognition and dynamic routing. Unlike traditional IVRs, modern AI-powered IVR solutions can understand customer requests, preserve context and enable seamless handoffs to human agents when required. Replacing legacy IVR systems can improve important metrics such as customer satisfaction, containment rates, average handling time and overall operational efficiency.
