Key takeaways Speech-to-speech AI removes unnecessary processing steps to create faster and more natural voice conversations. Response times below 250 milliseconds help conversations feel more human, improving customer engagement and reducing interruptions. Organisations adopting low-latency voice AI can improve customer satisfaction, agent productivity and operational efficiency.
