Key takeaways Strong voice AI ROI comes from reducing operating costs while improving customer satisfaction, agent productivity and business growth. Capture baseline metrics such as AHT, CSAT, FCR and cost per call before deployment to accurately measure success. When evaluating voice AI pricing, consider implementation, integrations, optimisation and long-term total cost of ownership, not just platform fees.
