Key takeaways The Enterprise Multi-Cloud Complexity Index (EMCI) helps organisations measure and understand the operational complexity of managing hybrid and multi-cloud environments. EMCI highlights gaps in governance, security, networking and multi-cloud management, enabling businesses to reduce risk and improve operational efficiency. A lower complexity score supports better performance, stronger cloud governance, lower costs and higher return on cloud investments.
