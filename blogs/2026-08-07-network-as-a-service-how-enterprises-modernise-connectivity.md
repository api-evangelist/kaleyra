Key takeaways Network as a Service (NaaS) delivers networking capabilities through a subscription-based model, eliminating the need for extensive on-premises infrastructure. NaaS enables businesses to scale bandwidth, deploy new sites, and connect cloud environments on demand. Software-defined networking and automation simplify network management while improving performance and resilience.
