What is multi-cloud connectivity in aviation? Multi-cloud connectivity refers to the ability to securely connect and enable data exchange between applications hosted across multiple cloud platforms. In the aviation industry, this is critical because airlines rely on a distributed ecosystem of cloud-based systems for reservations, inventory, flight operations, and customer engagement.
