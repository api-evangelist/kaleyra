What is a connected car ecosystem? A connected car ecosystem refers to the integration of vehicles, cloud platforms, networks, and applications to enable real-time data exchange and digital services. It allows automotive manufacturers to deliver features such as remote diagnostics, predictive maintenance, infotainment, and over-the-air (OTA) updates.
