In the modern digital landscape, organisations face constant pressure from fluctuating workloads. From sudden spikes in e-commerce traffic during major sales to intensive computing requirements during financial audits, static infrastructure often struggles to keep pace. This is where cloud bursting becomes a critical enabler.
