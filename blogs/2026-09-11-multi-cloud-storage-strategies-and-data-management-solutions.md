In today’s hyperconnected ecosystem, data has become both an enterprise’s most valuable asset and its greatest operational challenge. As organisations move beyond traditional connectivity models, storing data in a single, isolated silo is no longer viable. Digital excellence now depends on the ability to store, move, and manage data seamlessly across a digital fabric that spans multiple cloud providers, on-premises data centres, and edge locations.
