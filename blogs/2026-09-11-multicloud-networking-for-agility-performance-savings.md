Why enterprises are embracing the multicloud imperative Enterprises today are accelerating their shift towards hybrid and multicloud strategies to support digital transformation, improve resilience, and avoid vendor lock-in. Business-critical applications now run across a mix of private data centres and multiple public cloud platforms, each chosen for specific capabilities, cost efficiencies, or regional requirements.
