Business impact: Faster rollouts, lower costs, and stronger security Liberty General Insurance (LGI) achieved significant operational and financial benefits by modernising its branch connectivity approach. Using Security Service Edge (SSE), 33% of its branches were securely connected without the need for traditional, high-cost VPN installations. The new model delivered 50% cost savings compared to legacy Global VPN (GVPN) deployments, while reducing network delivery timelines from up to eight weeks to just three to four days.
