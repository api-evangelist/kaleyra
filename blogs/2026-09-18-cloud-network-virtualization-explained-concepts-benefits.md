The way enterprises design and operate networks is changing rapidly. For decades, networking was largely physical, built around cables, hardware appliances, and manual configurations that could take weeks or even months to deploy. In a world where businesses now operate across multiple clouds, regions, and geographies, that approach can no longer keep pace.
