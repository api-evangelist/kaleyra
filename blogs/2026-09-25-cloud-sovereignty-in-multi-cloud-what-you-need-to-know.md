In the modern digital landscape, businesses no longer rely on just one cloud provider. Most enterprises now operate across a complex web of environments, including AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP). While this multi-cloud approach offers flexibility, it also introduces a critical concern: Cloud Sovereignty.
