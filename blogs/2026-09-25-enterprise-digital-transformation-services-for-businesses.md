In the current global economy, enterprise digital transformation has evolved from a future-facing ambition into a critical business imperative. For modern organisations, the ability to pivot quickly, scale confidently, and innovate continuously depends on the strength of their digital foundation. As enterprises expand across multiple clouds and distributed data centres, traditional models of connectivity, security, and management increasingly fall short.
