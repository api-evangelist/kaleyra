In today’s enterprise environment, the discussion is no longer about whether organisations should adopt the cloud, but how they can effectively manage the many cloud environments they already rely on. As businesses decentralise operations and scale digitally, their “digital fabric” increasingly spans multiple environments, including AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud Platform (GCP), and private data centres. This shift towards multi-cloud offers clear advantages.
