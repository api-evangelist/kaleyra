In the modern digital economy, the shift towards multi-cloud architectures is no longer a strategic choice but a functional necessity. Enterprises are increasingly distributing workloads across multiple Cloud Service Providers (CSPs) to leverage specific platform strengths, reduce dependency on a single vendor, and improve global resilience. However, this architectural flexibility introduces a new challenge: operational complexity.
