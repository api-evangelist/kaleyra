In the current digital landscape, cloud migration is no longer a question of if, but how many. As enterprises increasingly adopt multi-cloud strategies to avoid vendor lock-in and leverage the unique strengths of different providers, the underlying network infrastructure is being stretched to its limits. Traditional connectivity models struggle to keep pace with the agility modern cloud environments demand.
